ABOUT THE CLUB

A CLUB BUILT ON COMMUNITY

Chirundu United F.C. is a professional football club representing Chirundu District in Lusaka Province. Known to supporters as the Border Boys, the club has built a passionate following of over 40,000 fans, backed by strong support from local leadership and the community.

On the pitch, the Border Boys compete for silverware; off it, the club stays close to the district it represents — sharing match reports, celebrations and community messages with supporters every week.
